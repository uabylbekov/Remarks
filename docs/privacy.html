Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

The short version: Remarks does not collect, track, sell, or share any of your personal data. Your notes belong strictly to you and stay on your devices and in your private iCloud account.

1. Data We Collect

None. Remarks does not include analytics, advertising frameworks, third-party crash reporters, or user-tracking SDKs. We do not operate any backend servers and never receive your notes, attachments, preferences, or usage data.

2. Local Storage & iCloud Sync

All notes, folders, tags, and attachments are stored locally on your device. If you are signed into iCloud, Remarks uses Apple's private CloudKit database to sync your library across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac, and Apple's iCloud Key-Value Store to sync appearance preferences. This data is stored under your personal Apple Account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy; the developer has no access to it.

3. Offline Rendering

Markdown formatting, syntax highlighting, LaTeX math equations (KaTeX), and diagrams (Mermaid) are rendered entirely on-device using bundled local resources. Your note contents are never transmitted to any external rendering service.

4. Device Permissions & System Features

  • Camera: Requested only if you choose to take a photo inside Remarks to attach to a note. Captured photos are saved solely inside your note library.
  • Photos & Files: Remarks uses Apple's system photo picker and file importer, accessing only the specific items you explicitly select to attach or import.
  • Spotlight & Siri / Shortcuts: By default, Remarks indexes note titles, short previews, and tags locally on your device so you can find notes via Spotlight and Siri. Full note bodies are never shared with Spotlight, and you can disable indexing at any time in Settings → Search → Show Notes in Spotlight.

5. Deleting Your Data

You can delete any note or folder inside Remarks at any time. Deleted notes move to Recently Deleted and are permanently removed after 30 days (or immediately when you choose Delete Permanently). Uninstalling Remarks removes all local data from your device; iCloud data can be managed in your device's iCloud settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Because Remarks collects no personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is ever updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact
