Frequently Asked Questions

How does iCloud sync work?

Remarks syncs automatically through your personal iCloud account—no separate account is required. If a device isn't syncing, confirm that:

  • You are signed into the same Apple Account on all devices.
  • iCloud Drive / iCloud sync is enabled for Remarks in System Settings (Mac) or Settings → Apple Account → iCloud (iPhone/iPad).
  • Your iCloud account has available storage space.

You can check the current sync state at the bottom of the sidebar.

How do I write math equations and Mermaid diagrams?

  • Inline math: Wrap LaTeX in single dollar signs, e.g. $E = mc^2$.
  • Display equations: Wrap multi-line LaTeX in $$ ... $$.
  • Mermaid diagrams: Use a fenced code block starting with ```mermaid.

All math and diagrams render completely offline on your device.

How do I export my notes?

Open any note and choose Export as → Markdown… to save a portable folder containing the .md file and an attachments/ folder with relative links.

How do I turn off Spotlight indexing?

Open Settings inside Remarks and toggle off Show Notes in Spotlight. Existing Spotlight entries for your notes are cleared immediately.

Can I use Remarks on iPhone, iPad, and Mac with one purchase?

Yes. Remarks is a Universal Purchase—buying it once on the App Store unlocks it on your iPhone, iPad, and Mac signed into the same Apple Account.
