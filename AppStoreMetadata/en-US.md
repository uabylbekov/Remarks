## Description
```
Remarks is a fast, native Markdown notes app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac—built for clear writing, code, math, and diagrams.

Pay once. No subscriptions. No accounts.
Your notes stay on your devices and sync privately through your own iCloud account.

Designed for Technical & Everyday Notes
• Live-preview Markdown editor for headings, lists, checklists, quotes, links, and inline formatting
• Syntax-highlighted code blocks with bundled monospace fonts (Fira Code, JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono), ligatures, and adaptive light/dark themes
• LaTeX math equations rendered with KaTeX
• Mermaid diagrams rendered right inside your notes
• Native interactive tables—insert or remove rows and columns effortlessly
• Photo and file attachments with clean Markdown export

Organized & Native to Apple Platforms
• Nested folders, tags, pinned notes, instant search, and 30-day Recently Deleted recovery
• Spotlight and Siri / Shortcuts support (titles, previews, and tags only—full note text is never shared)
• Works completely offline and syncs automatically with iCloud
```
