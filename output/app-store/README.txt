Remarks App Store screenshots

Intended order: 01 Kyoto travel note, 02 populated library, 03 calculus equations, 04 code and diagram, 05 coffee in dark mode.

Only screenshots in the upload-ready archive are ready for App Store Connect. drafts/mac contains incomplete native window exports with missing toolbar/sidebar content; do not upload those.

Generated photographic assets and their prompts are in assets/. The app uses them only in its DEBUG screenshot seed, launched with -ScreenshotDemo (in-memory data).

Mac: 2560x1600 window captures in mac/ and upload-ready/mac/. Dock hidden and system dark mode toggled during capture, both restored. The Mac app takes about 20s to show its window after launch.
Duo: inner-display (2853x2007) captures in iphone-duo/ and upload-ready/iphone-duo/. Taken via DEBUG launch args -ScreenshotNote <title> and -ScreenshotColumns all|double.

Apple specifications: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications
